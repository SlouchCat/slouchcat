# Suffixes of audiobook series names (concept 19 D4.1). The series key leaves them out, and the
# displayed name of a series is the most common spelling without them, so «Bodyguard - Band» and
# «Bodyguard» are one series called «Bodyguard». One suffix per line, compared as whole words
# without case; hyphens, dashes and brackets count as spaces («- Band», «-Reihe» and «[German
# Edition]» are covered by «band», «reihe» and «german edition»). A line ending in «…» cuts the
# name at the first occurrence of its words («gesprochen von …»: everything from «gesprochen von»
# on). Lines starting with # and empty lines are ignored. Changing this list changes keys, never
# the id of a series (D4.4).
