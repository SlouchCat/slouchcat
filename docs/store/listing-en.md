## Full description

```text
Slouchcat is the app for your own Slouchcat server. The server runs in your home or on a machine you operate and manages your own media collection: films, series, audiobooks and podcasts. The app lets you watch and listen to them on your phone and tablet.

The app contains no content and offers none. It needs a Slouchcat server that you set up yourself or that someone invited you to.

BROWSE
• Home screen with the rows of your server: continue watching, new, picks of the household
• Libraries with filters and sorting, search, people, collections, watchlist
• Profiles for every member of the household, with PIN and an age limit the server enforces

PLAY
• Video player with audio tracks, subtitles, chapters and preview images while seeking
• Skip intros, next episode, resume where you left off
• The server picks the right playback for your device and converts where needed
• Audiobooks and podcasts with chapters, speed and playback with the screen off
• Web radio stations your server offers

OFFLINE
• Save films, episodes and audiobooks from your server on the device and play them without a network
• Save the next episodes automatically, on Wi-Fi only if you like
• Progress syncs the next time the device reaches the server

CONNECT A TV
• Set up the Slouchcat app on an Android TV device by scanning its QR code, without typing a password on the TV

NOTIFICATIONS
• Notifications from your server, for example about new episodes, even when the app is closed

YOUR DATA
The app talks to your server and to nobody else. Account, collection and progress stay on your server. No ads, no analytics, no account with the developer. Two small helper services of the project only pass on encrypted messages: one when you set up a TV, one for notifications. Details in the privacy policy.

FREE SOFTWARE
Server and app are free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3. Source code and server guide: https://github.com/SlouchCat/slouchcat
```
